Sacred Significance

Ganesh Chaturthi marks the divine descent of Lord Ganesha to Earth. This Seva at the historic Siddhivinayak Temple is performed with strict Vedic protocols to invoke 'Siddhi' (spiritual power) and 'Riddhi' (prosperity). Worshipping the Elephant-headed God at the start of any new venture ensures the removal of karmic hurdles.

The ritual involves 'Sahasranama' (1000 names) chanting and 'Abhishek' with holy waters from seven sacred rivers, creating a powerful spiritual vibration that resonates within the devotee's life.
