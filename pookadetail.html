Significance & Benefits

Ganesh Chaturthi marks the birth of Lord Ganesha, the lord of wisdom and the destroyer of obstacles. Performing this pooja with devotion is believed to bring peace, prosperity, and success to the devotee's household. In the Vedic tradition, Ganesha is worshipped first before any other deity to ensure the smooth completion of rituals.

Pooja Vidhi (Procedure)

1

Ganpati Sthapana

The ritual begins with the installation of the Ganesha idol on a raised platform decorated with flowers and rangoli.

2

Prana Pratishtha

Chanting of sacred Vedic mantras to invoke the divine life force into the deity.

3

Shodashopachara

The sixteen ways of paying tribute, including offering Durva grass, Modaks, and red flowers.

4

Uttar Pooja & Aarti

The concluding ritual followed by a grand Mangal Aarti and distribution of Prasad.
