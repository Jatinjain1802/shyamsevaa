# Razorpay Onboarding – Documentation Checklist (India)

This document outlines the required KYC and business documents for onboarding an Indian business on Razorpay Payment Gateway.

> Note: All documents must be clear, valid, and match the legal name of the business/entity.

---

# 1. Basic Documents Required (All Business Types)

## 1.1 PAN Card

- **Business PAN** (for Company/LLP/Partnership/Trust)
- **Individual PAN** (for Sole Proprietorship)

PAN details must exactly match the business/entity name.

---

## 1.2 Identity Proof (Authorized Signatory / Proprietor / Directors)

Any one of the following:

- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Voter ID
- Driving License

---

## 1.3 Address Proof (Authorized Signatory / Proprietor)

Any one of the following:

- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Voter ID
- Driving License

---

## 1.4 Bank Account Proof (Mandatory)

Bank account must be in the name of the business/entity.

Provide any one:

- Cancelled cheque (with business name printed)
- Bank statement (recent, showing account holder name, IFSC, and account number)
- Bank passbook copy (with account details)

---

# 2. Proof of Business Existence

Provide any one valid business proof:

- GST Registration Certificate
- Certificate of Incorporation (for Companies/LLP)
- Partnership Deed (for Partnership firms)
- Shop & Establishment Certificate
- MSME / Udyam Registration Certificate
- Trade License

---

# 3. If the Business DOES NOT Have GST Registration

GST is NOT mandatory if the business is not required to register under GST law (e.g., turnover below threshold or exempt category).

In such cases, provide ANY ONE of the following instead:

- Shop & Establishment Certificate
- Udyam (MSME) Registration Certificate
- Trade License
- Professional Tax Registration
- Business Registration Certificate issued by local authority
- Import Export Code (IEC), if applicable

Additionally:

- A declaration may be required stating that the business is not liable for GST registration.

---

# 4. Entity-Specific Document Requirements

## 4.1 Sole Proprietorship

- Proprietor PAN
- Address proof of proprietor
- Business bank account proof
- Any one business proof (GST / Shop & Establishment / MSME / Trade License)

---

## 4.2 Partnership Firm

- Partnership Deed
- Firm PAN
- Authorized partner PAN & ID proof
- Bank account proof (in firm name)

---

## 4.3 Private Limited Company

- Certificate of Incorporation
- Company PAN
- Memorandum & Articles of Association (if requested)
- Board Resolution authorizing signatory
- Authorized Signatory PAN & ID proof
- Company bank account proof

---

## 4.4 LLP (Limited Liability Partnership)

- Certificate of Incorporation
- LLP Agreement
- LLP PAN
- Designated Partner PAN & ID proof
- Bank account proof (in LLP name)

---

## 4.5 Trust / HUF

- Trust Deed / HUF Deed
- PAN Card
- Authorized Signatory PAN & ID proof
- Bank account proof

---

# 5. Important Compliance Notes

- Bank account name must match the business/entity name.
- PAN details must match MCA or registration records.
- Mobile number and email used for signup should belong to the authorized signatory.
- All documents must be valid and not expired.
- Razorpay may request additional documents depending on business category.

---

# 6. Razorpay Onboarding Steps

1. Sign up on Razorpay Dashboard using email and mobile number.
2. Enter business details (entity type, industry, address, website/app details).
3. Submit KYC documents.
4. Razorpay performs verification.
5. Account gets activated upon successful verification.
6. Generate API keys and integrate payment gateway.
